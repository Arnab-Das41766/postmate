--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5: THE 4-TIER CRITICALITY SAFETY SYSTEM (Risk Interception)
Demonstrates: Autonomous safety rules, prompt confirmations, risk explanation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Demo Tier 3: CRITICAL (Requires 'yes' / 'no')]
Add a new clinic called City Health Clinic at point 86.20, 22.80

  * What happens: Postmate prints the SQL with a yellow [CRITICAL] badge
    and pauses for a `yes/no` confirmation before writing data.
  * Action: Type `no` to show safe cancellation.

[Demo Tier 4: VERY CRITICAL (Requires typing 'CONFIRM')]
Delete all records from osm_points

  * What happens: Postmate intercepts the query with a red [VERY CRITICAL]
    badge, displays a plain English risk explanation (deletes all rows),
    and strictly demands typing `CONFIRM` to execute.
  * Action: Press Enter or type `cancel` to show that accidental execution
    is impossible!
